# 6. Results

- **Three headline numbers:** recovered per rupee disputed, win rate when fought, and answered on time.
- **"Was the advisor right?"** Labelled Expected and Won bars with a verdict chip, plus separate Fold and Escalate cards.
- **By-reason bars,** with the weakest reason named.
- **What to change next:** one AI suggestion from the counts, with a saved example.
- **Dispute ratio tile:** two typed numbers, with Razorpay's attributed figures (0.5% recommended, 0.75% emergency controls, Visa 1.5% from April 2026).
- **Sample history** (24 made-up disputes, labelled as such) with an off switch, and a ledger of your own actions kept in the browser.
- **Dispute ratio** takes whole numbers with commas ("1,200"), refuses "1e3", decimals and negatives with a clear message, and says so when disputes exceed payments.
- **Was the advisor right?** counts only Fight calls you actually fought; the note says your own actions count as answered on time.
