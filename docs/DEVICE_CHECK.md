# Device check (about 15 minutes in total)

You are checking that the live link looks and works on devices I cannot test here. You only need the public link, nothing to install.

| Device | What to open | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone, Safari | The live link, the disputes list, C06, C15, Results, Evals | Nothing cut off sideways; the call chip and the main button visible without scrolling; the bar at the bottom of C06 does not cover the text; tapping the tour opens it and Next works |
| Mac or Windows, Firefox | The same pages | Same look as Chrome; the Evals page opens; tab through the page once with the Tab key and check there is a visible outline |
| Mac, Safari (if you have one) | The same pages | Same as above |

Do these three things on each device:
1. Open C06 and tap "Run live check" (it may show a saved result; that is fine).
2. Open C15 and tap "Under the hood".
3. Take the tour from the yellow banner.

If anything looks wrong, take a screenshot and send it with the device name. One person who has never seen the app can do the same on their own phone: ask them to open the link and tell you, aloud, what they think the product does. Do not help them.
